# Full Repository Architectural Audit

Objective:

Perform a complete architectural audit of the repository using CodeBroker.

Your goal is to determine whether CodeBroker can accurately explain a large repository without requiring significant manual exploration.

Requirements:

1. Determine the overall purpose of the repository.
2. Identify all major subsystems.
3. Identify repository entrypoints.
4. Identify architectural hotspots.
5. Identify the most depended-on symbols.
6. Identify the most critical files.
7. Identify dependency cycles.
8. Identify architectural bottlenecks.
9. Identify large dependency trees.
10. Explain how major subsystems communicate.

Mandatory CodeBroker Tools:

* get_workspace
* project_overview
* project_overview_ai
* repository_stats
* architectural_hotspots
* dependency_cycles
* graph_subtree
* subsystem_stats
* subsystem_overview

Evaluation Questions:

* Was repository understanding accurate?
* Were hotspots useful?
* Were subsystem boundaries clear?
* Were dependency cycles meaningful?
* Did CodeBroker eliminate manual exploration?
* How many tokens were saved?

Generate a benchmark report when complete.
