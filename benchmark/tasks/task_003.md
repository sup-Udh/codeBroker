# Deep Dependency Investigation

Objective:

Investigate the relationship between two major systems in the repository.

Your goal is to evaluate graph quality, dependency tracing accuracy, and architectural understanding.

Requirements:

1. Identify two major subsystems.
2. Discover how they communicate.
3. Find the shortest dependency path.
4. Explore graph neighborhoods around both systems.
5. Identify critical connector symbols.
6. Identify shared infrastructure.
7. Identify failure points.
8. Identify hidden dependencies.
9. Explain the overall interaction model.

Mandatory CodeBroker Tools:

* shortest_path
* explore_graph
* graph_subtree
* get_context
* get_implementation
* architectural_hotspots
* subsystem_stats

Evaluation Questions:

* Was the shortest path accurate?
* Were graph traversals useful?
* Were dependencies understandable?
* Did graph exploration reveal useful architecture?
* Were there missing graph relationships?

Generate a benchmark report when complete.
